# 🚀 OmniGuard: Multimodal AI-Based Authenticity and Fraud Detection System

## 📌 Overview

OmniGuard is an AI-powered multimodal fraud detection platform designed to identify suspicious, manipulated, or fake digital content across multiple data formats including:

- 📩 Text
- 🖼️ Images
- 🎥 Videos

The system uses Machine Learning, Deep Learning, NLP, and Computer Vision techniques to analyze uploaded content and determine whether it is authentic or fraudulent.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

With the rapid growth of Artificial Intelligence, fake digital content such as:

- phishing messages
- scam emails
- fake reviews
- deepfake videos
- AI-generated visuals

has become increasingly common.

Many users struggle to identify whether online content is genuine or manipulated, leading to:

- financial fraud
- misinformation
- identity theft
- fake job scams

OmniGuard aims to solve this problem using AI-driven authenticity verification.
